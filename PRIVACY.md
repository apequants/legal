# Ape Quants Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 23, 2026.*

Ape Quants is a game on Reddit run by Whimsy Ink LLC ("we"). The short version: we keep what the game needs to run your rides, your streak and the boards. We keep it inside Reddit, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone.

## 1. What we keep

**When you play**, in the storage Reddit gives apps for the community where you play:
- your Reddit user ID (a code like t2_abc123) and your Reddit username, which the game shows on its boards;
- your record: your streak and streak freezes, how many rides you've finished, your Moons, your Island level, your best amount, and whether you've seen FOUNDER MODE's how-it-works card;
- your rides: the market, how much you put in, each flip, when you sold, how it ended, whether you beat Jane Lane and whether you shared it. A ride's details are kept 30 days, and its result stays on the boards;
- the desks you open: the market, the odds you set, the words you picked from the game's lists, the bank, how it's doing, and its post;
- your places on the boards (TODAY, REKT, the Moon Wall, TOP DESKS and DESK BREAKERS), and your lines in the ticker (your username and how your ride went), which go after three days;
- which days and desks you've played, so that deleting your data can find every board you're on, and when the game last saw you, for the deleted-account check (section 3);
- short markers so one tap only counts once (kept for seconds), and so you ride another ape's desk once a day (kept two days).

**On your device** (your browser's storage): whether the game's sound is on or off, and, on HURRICANE COVER, how much you chose to put in on the post, so the game opens with it.

## 2. Where it's kept, and who sees it

Everything lives in the storage Reddit provides to apps on its Developer Platform. Reddit, Inc. hosts the game and runs that storage and the servers. Reddit also collects its own information when you use Reddit, including this game, under Reddit's own Privacy Policy. We don't control what Reddit collects or how it uses it.

Each community that has the game keeps its own copy of what's above.

**What other players see:** your username, your results and your places on the boards, in the ticker, and on the post of any desk you open. When you tap SHARE and confirm, the game posts your result as a comment from your account, public on Reddit like any comment you write. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone, except where the law requires it.

## 3. How long we keep it

- **Your record and your places on the boards** are kept while the game is running, so they're there when you come back.
- **A ride's details** are kept 30 days.
- **If you delete your Ape Quants data**, it's removed at once from the community where you asked. If you've played in other communities, delete it there too. Desks you opened stay open for the apes riding them but lose your name, and their posts are deleted, since a desk post's title names its maker.
- **If you delete your Reddit account**, a regular check notices and removes everything we hold for that account within 30 days. The check asks Reddit about players the game hasn't seen for a week. Reddit gives the same answer for suspended accounts, so the check waits for three answers a week apart, and any visit to the game starts it over.
- **Comments** you shared stay on Reddit, where you or the moderators can delete them. If a post or comment is deleted, the game forgets its link to it.
- **If the game is retired**, the data goes with it.

## 4. Your choices

- **Delete your data yourself, any time:** open the ⋯ menu on any Ape Quants post, choose "Delete my Ape Quants data" and confirm. Next time you play, you start as a new ape.
- **Ask us** what we hold about you, or to delete it for you: choose "Report a problem with Ape Quant" in the same menu, or message the moderators of r/ApeQuants from your account. Moderators can delete a player's data by their username. We'll answer within 30 days.
- **If you're in the EU or UK:** we use your data to run the game you chose to play, and the boards and the one-tap markers for our legitimate interest in running a fair game. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

## 5. Children

Ape Quants is for people old enough to use Reddit. We don't knowingly keep information about children under 13. If you believe a child's data is saved, tell the moderators and we'll delete it.
